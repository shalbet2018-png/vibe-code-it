WELCOME TO MOKE GARDENS

Moke gardens is an exclusive gated community offering stunning and luxurious surroundings, located at the foot of the magnificent Lukenya hills and just 35 kilometers from the Nairobi Central Business District. Some of the social amenities include the Jommo Kenyatta International Airport which is only a ten-minute drive from Moke Gardens, Daystar University and The Nairobi Expressway a mere 5 kilometers away. 

Other than the spectacular 4bed room Maisonettes with a DSQ, Moke Gardens Ltd has partnered with the Government of Kenya under the Affordable Housing Projects, to develop affordable and sustainable apartments.

THE APARTMENTS

The apartments comprising of 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms units have state of the art designs with the best interest of the client at the heart of it. The apartments are well clustered and will have plenty of greenery space for recreation. With ease of access and in a quiet location, this is an ideal investment for home owners looking for a decent, quality and an affordable home.

Construction of the apartments began in October 2021 and the first blocks of apartments are expected to be completed by the end of 2027, with a total of 58 housing units

THE MAISONETTES

The semi-detached maisonettes are a marvel of architecture with exquisite finishes such as hardwood railing on the staircase and wood flooring (optional). The houses comprise of 3 bedrooms all being en-suite on the upper level with a guest room on the lower level. The maisonette also includes an attached DSQ with a separate entrance. 

The maisonettes front has a landscaped private garden and parking for two while the back has a huge landscaped private garden idea for barbecue and parties.  The maisonettes sector has a perimeter wall with barbed wire and a 24 hours manned gate.

 90% of the 4 bedroom maisonettes are completed and sold out and only 10% of the maisonettes remain.
