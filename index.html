Affordable Housing

Alongside the spectacular 4 bedroom maisonettes with DSQ, Moke Gardens Ltd has partnered with the Government of Kenya under the Affordable Housing Programme to develop affordable and sustainable apartments.

Construction began in October 2021, and the first blocks are expected to be completed by the end of 2027, with a total of 58 housing units.
