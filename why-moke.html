Why Moke Gardens

Discover a comfortable home in the scenic Lukenya area, with access to Nairobi and the amenities of Athi River.

Illustrative visual

Affordability

Through the Government of Kenya’s Affordable Housing Projects initiative, Moke Gardens offers quality apartment homes at competitive rates in the Mavoko area.

Accessibility

Enjoy access to Nairobi and nearby social amenities while coming home to a quieter setting. Athi River is served by rail connections and is close to Daystar University, Lukenya Getaway, Pine Breeze Getaway and Small World Country Club.

Nature

The development is designed to complement the serene surroundings, with spacious windows connecting interiors to the outdoors and supporting natural ventilation.
