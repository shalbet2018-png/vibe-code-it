The Apartments

Decent, modern and affordable. The apartments comprise studio, one, two and three bedroom homes with practical layouts and residents' needs at heart. Landscaped green spaces, a quiet location and convenient access make Moke Gardens a welcoming place to call home.

Moke Gardens is part of the Government of Kenya Affordable Housing Programme, with a target of 3,000 housing units.
