Our Story

Lukenya is an emerging area for home buyers and investors. As infrastructure develops and the area connects with Nairobi’s economy, Moke Gardens was created to bring homes to this growing neighbourhood while preserving a peaceful setting. The gated-community concept reflects a commitment to security and residents’ comfort.
